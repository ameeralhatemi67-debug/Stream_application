Watch educational broadcasts and recordings, follow channels, and find lecture venues in Al Khobar, Dhahran and Dammam.

lectures,education,live,learning,venues,Khobar,Dhahran,Dammam,Arabic

## Description

Hadayah Live brings educational live broadcasts, recorded lectures and the places that host them into one app, in Arabic and English.

WATCH AND DISCOVER
Browse channels, recorded lectures and playlists. Open a live room to watch, join the conversation and send reactions. Follow channels and save recordings or upcoming broadcasts to return to later. Scheduled broadcasts show their time in Saudi time.

FIND LECTURE VENUES
Explore venues in Al Khobar, Dhahran and Dammam. View a venue on the map and open directions in your maps app.

EXPLORE ORGANIZATIONS
Discover participating organizations, their public pages, teams, venues and scheduled broadcasts.

YOUR ACCOUNT AND SAFETY
Browse and watch as a guest. Sign in with Apple or Google for chat, follows and saved content. Report messages, block users and manage your account and privacy in Settings.

An Apple-linked account is deleted inside the iOS app with fresh Apple authorization. If deletion is blocked, the account has not been deleted; email support to ask what is blocking it. Support cannot complete deletion by email alone. Phone publishing, channel connection and push notifications are not available in this iOS release.

Hadayah Live uses YouTube API Services and the YouTube player. Use of these services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
