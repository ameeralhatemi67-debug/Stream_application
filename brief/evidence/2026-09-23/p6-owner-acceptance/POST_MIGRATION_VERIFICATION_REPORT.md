## 5. Summary of Onboarding / Role Selection Defect

* **Symptom:** Inconsistent prompting of *"Want to be a visitor or streamer?"* (`/role-select`) upon sign-in.
* **Root Cause Identified:** `AppProvider._hasCompletedRoleSelection` is an in-memory boolean in RAM that resets to `false` on every reload/reboot. Non-streamer profiles (`is_streamer = false`) fail the router gate `if (!provider.hasCompletedRoleSelection && !provider.isApprovedStreamer)` and are forced back to `/role-select`.
* **Fix Target:** Persist role selection state in `SharedPreferences` keyed to user ID.
