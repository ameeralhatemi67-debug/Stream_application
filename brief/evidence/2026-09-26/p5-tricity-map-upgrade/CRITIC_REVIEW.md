# Independent critic reviews

Rules (from `brief/research/p5-tricity-map-upgrade/OPUS_EXECUTION_PROMPT.md`): at most **three** critic reviews for the whole map upgrade. Four categories scored 0-10 by the critic, not the implementer: Functionality; Accessibility and platform compatibility; Integration and connectivity; Ease of use. Below 8 fails a category. Critical/high defects, unlicensed assets, failed G0 gates or missing essential acceptance evidence block acceptance regardless of scores. Missing physical evidence is provisional, not "verified good". Each round is tied to the reviewed commit; any later source fix invalidates affected evidence until re-checked.

Rounds used: 2 of 3 (round 1: NEEDS WORK; round 2: NEEDS WORK)

### Confirmed good (independently checked)

- **Local range client** (`local_pmtiles.dart:32-62`): only GET to the one internal URI, single `bytes=a-b`, 416 past EOF, 4 MiB cap, 206 + Content-Range, closed on dispose; the provider disables the disk cache and keys caches by pack SHA. No server, fork or parser.
- **Pack verification** (`map_pack_controller.dart:171-245`): schema 1, native zoom 0-15, per-file size + SHA-256 for pack and both styles, local-only style rule, header zoom re-check; any failure is `unavailable` with a coded problem, never a partial map. `ensureOpened` is only called from `initState` (no rebuild retry loop).
- **Pack data**: `pack-validation.json` really shows 8,298/8,298 slots, 0 missing, 0 decode errors, per-zoom counts and named control tiles with road/building/POI counts; manifest hashes match; the extract was a ~12 MB regional read (no planet download).
- **Viewport policy math** (`map_viewport_policy.dart`): containment clamps the centre by half a viewport in Mercator space; `minZoom = max(contain, overviewFit)`; feasible-canvas shrink; per-frame clamping in `_moveTo` (`spatial_map_screen.dart:121-149`). Every programmatic path I traced ends in `_moveTo`: overview (`:166`), dropdown and city search (`:172`), venue focus/list/search (`:186-207`, `:543-552`), cluster tap (`:355-365`), marker double-tap (`:446`, `:1066`), resize/tab restore (`:154`, `:531-537`). Gestures, wheel, keyboard and the built-in double-tap use `CameraConstraint.contain(navigation)` plus `minZoom/maxZoom` (`:584-595`), rotation off. The picker uses the same policy (`location_picker_modal.dart:108-126, 203-229`).
- **Domain filter and data truth**: `isInTricityMapDomain` rejects non-finite, (0,0) and out-of-rectangle points; applied to both current and cached pins (`map_visible_catalog.dart`). Records are not mutated. Moderation (verified/hidden), null-vs-empty snapshot, cached-marker ID reconciliation and the `isOnline`-gated live layer are unchanged in the diff. Directions keep exact coordinates and now show a localized failure.
- **Service worker safety** (`web/streamer_offline_sw.js`): it never writes to the cache, ignores non-GET and every origin except same-origin and the two gstatic CDNs, is network-first, and is bypassed by preparation's `cache:'reload'`.
