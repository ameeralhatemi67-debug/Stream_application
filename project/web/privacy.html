Privacy Policy

Hadayah Live (منصة هدايه) · Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Contact: ameeralhatemi67@gmail.com

1. Who we are

Hadayah Live is an educational live-broadcasting and discovery app for lectures, venues and organizations, available on Android and on the web. If you use the iOS app, this policy covers it too. The iOS app is for watching and account features: it does not offer phone broadcasting, YouTube channel connection or push notifications. This policy explains what personal data the app handles and why. For any privacy question or request, write to the contact address above.

2. What we collect

  • Account data. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID. If you use the iOS app and sign in with Apple, we receive a stable Apple user ID and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. Apple may give us a private relay address that forwards to your inbox instead of your real email, and it shares your name only the first time you sign in to the app with Apple. We never see your Google or Apple password. You can also use the app as a guest without an account.
  • Profile content you add. Display name, bio, avatar, banner and your YouTube channel handle.
  • Broadcaster and organization applications. Name, email, phone number, academic or organization details, YouTube handle, venue details and the images you upload. Administrators add review notes.
  • Venue location. A venue point you confirm on the map is saved and shown publicly on the map. If you tap "use my location" the app reads your precise location once, only to place the map pin on your device. Nothing is sent until you confirm a point, and only that venue point is saved. The app never reads your location in the background.
  • Chat and safety data. Chat messages you send, reports you file, accounts you block, and mutes or bans applied to you, with the related moderation records.
  • Your activity. Channels you follow, recordings and upcoming streams you save, reminders you set and notification preferences.
  • Device and notification data. A device identifier, device name and platform, the time the device was last active, a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging, on Android and the web) and live viewer presence, used to keep the live viewer count accurate and to deliver notifications. The iOS app does not register for push notifications, so it sends no push token.
  • Consent record. The version of this policy you accepted and when.
  • Camera and microphone. Only when an approved broadcaster starts a phone broadcast on Android. Live video and audio are sent directly to the broadcaster's YouTube live ingest. We do not record or store them. The iOS app does not offer phone broadcasting; if that changes, we will update this policy before it is offered.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, our providers (section 5) process your IP address and connection details to deliver the service.

3. Google and YouTube data

A broadcaster (an individual, or an organization owner) can connect their own YouTube channel on Android or the web. Channel connection is not available in the iOS app. This uses Google sign-in with the sensitive scope youtube.force-ssl, which lets our server, on the broadcaster's behalf, create the live broadcast and its stream, link them, start, end and check them, and read the channel's identity (ID and title).

  • We never see your Google password.
  • The access credentials are stored on the server in an encrypted secrets vault, are never sent to the app or to other users, and are used only for the actions above.
  • You can disconnect the channel inside the app, or revoke access at any time in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting your account destroys the stored credentials.
  • Hadayah Live's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide the broadcasting features you request. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it except where you ask for support, for security, or where the law requires.
  • Videos play through the YouTube embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube controls that player and may show ads in it. YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it collects. Using youtube-nocookie.com does not mean YouTube collects nothing.

4. How we use data

  • To run the app: sign-in, profiles, discovery, the venue map, live rooms, chat, follows, bookmarks, reminders and notifications.
  • To review broadcaster and organization applications and to run organization tools (members, roles, invitations, schedules and shows).
  • To keep the community safe: moderation, reports, blocks, mutes and bans.
  • To show administrators combined viewing and engagement statistics so they can run the service. [TO CONFIRM — owner/counsel: the purposes these statistics are used for]
  • To keep the service reliable and secure, and to comply with the law.

We do not sell personal data. The app does not run its own ads or include an advertising SDK. Videos are played by YouTube, which may show its own ads (see section 3).

5. Who we share data with

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and server functions). Our project is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany (AWS eu-central-1), so your data is stored outside Saudi Arabia.
  • Google (Google sign-in, the YouTube API and player, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications on Android and the web).
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, if you use the iOS app). When you delete an Apple-linked account, our server sends Apple a fresh authorization from you so that Apple revokes the app's sign-in. It uses those Apple credentials only for that step and does not store them.
  • Vercel (hosting of the web app and a server endpoint that reads public YouTube information).
  • Map and image providers may receive your IP address and the map area you view when tiles or images load.

What other users can see: your display name, avatar and profile, chat messages in the room where you post them, and organization and venue pages and coordinates you publish. Administrators and moderators can see reports and moderation records. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and copy. Settings → Privacy → export my data saves a JSON file of the data linked to your account.
  • Delete. Settings → Account & Data → Delete My Account deletes your account and linked data. The app first checks whether something must be done: end a live broadcast, transfer ownership of an organization you own that has other members (or remove them), or email us about a restricted account. Deletion runs as separate steps (your uploaded files are removed, then the account is deleted). If a step fails, the app tells you and your account is kept, though some files may already be removed; you can try again. Without the app, use the deletion request page; we respond within 30 days. [TO CONFIRM — owner: support can meet this 30-day response time]
  • Apple-linked accounts. In the iOS app, deleting an account linked to Apple asks you for a fresh Apple authorization, and our server must revoke the app's Apple sign-in before the account is deleted. If that step is unavailable, cancelled or fails, the account is kept and the app explains how to retry or contact support. [TO CONFIRM — owner: the Apple revocation server function is deployed and tested on a real iPhone before the iOS app is submitted]
  • Correct. Edit your profile in the app, or write to us.
  • Withdraw consent. Delete your account, or write to us (an Apple-linked account still has to be deleted in the iOS app, as below). Notifications and location can also be switched off in your phone's settings, and notification categories inside the app.
  • Proving it is you. Support may ask you to confirm that you own the account, but will never ask for your Google or Apple password or sign-in codes.
  • You may also contact us to exercise any other right you have under applicable law, including the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law.

Apple-linked accounts: this limit applies to every deletion request made outside the iOS app. An account linked to Apple can only be fully deleted inside the iOS app with fresh Apple authorization. Support cannot complete deletion of an Apple-linked account by email alone. Your account has not been deleted. Email support to ask what is blocking it.

7. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When deletion completes, your profile and linked data are removed, uploaded files are deleted, and your name and email are removed from moderation and review records; the rest of that operational history can remain. Chat messages you sent are deleted with the account.

Our providers' logs and backups have their own expiry schedules and may keep copies for a limited time. [TO CONFIRM — owner/counsel: log and backup retention periods, and any exceptions the law requires] Deleting your account does not delete your Google or Apple account, your videos on YouTube, or copies, exports and screenshots that you or other people already made.

If you delete an Apple-linked account, our server keeps a private record that the Apple revocation succeeded (which Apple identity, its version and the time). The record is valid for ten minutes and cannot authorize a deletion after that. It is removed with the account; if the account is not deleted, the record can stay until you try again or the account is deleted.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to each table is limited by database access rules, so people can read only what they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account is at risk, contact us.

9. Children

Hadayah Live is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. The app has no age verification or parental controls. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. An Apple-linked account has to be deleted in the iOS app (section 6), and we will explain how.

10. Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes, and we will tell you in the app when a change is significant.
