Map data

Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The bundled vector tiles (basemap.pmtiles) are a regional extract of the
Protomaps Basemap build 20260926 (schema 4.15.2; OpenStreetMap replication
2026-09-26 04:00 UTC), produced from OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth data.
Protomaps tiles are a Produced Work of the OpenStreetMap database:
https://protomaps.com/legal  https://docs.protomaps.com/basemaps/downloads
Natural Earth data is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Regional extract: pmtiles 1.31.2 `extract` over
bbox 49.70,25.95,50.45,26.80 (west,south,east,north), zoom 0-15.
Build and validation steps: project/tool/maps/README.md in the app source.

Map style
---------
style-en.json and style-ar.json were written for this app
(project/tool/maps/build_styles.py) against the Protomaps basemap v4 schema.
No third-party style, sprite or glyph files are bundled.

City outlines
-------------

choose a map view; they are not official municipal limits.
