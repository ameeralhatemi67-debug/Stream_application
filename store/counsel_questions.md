# Questions for Saudi and store counsel

Status: 2026-09-24. Questions only; no legal conclusion. Each answer and resulting policy text is **for counsel review**. The owner should attach the final data-flow map, actual operator identity, target market, host contracts and product scope before counsel answers.

1. CST: does this service's discovery of third-party YouTube lectures, in-app chat, user profiles and broadcaster-initiated phone RTMP fall within a digital-content-platform category requiring a CST license, registration or notification? Which entity applies, and when? CST says in-scope local and international providers must seek the relevant regulatory tool; it lists video sharing, social media and related services. [CST regulations](https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/regulations-and-licenses/regulations/Document-1549) [CST scope notice](https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/CST-Announces-The-Deadline-for-Complying-with-the-Regulations-for-Providing-Digital-Content-Platfor)
2. General Authority of Media Regulation, formerly GCAM: does the platform, its broadcasters or both need an audiovisual-media license, and which service category? Does embedding YouTube, providing a discovery feed or ingesting to a creator's YouTube channel change the answer? Do not infer that an IPTV or broadcasting license named on a government portal automatically applies. [Saudi audiovisual-media law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/ed5fdbc0-c183-4a8a-a8b7-a9ed004b5900/1) [Saudi government authority page](https://my.gov.sa/en/agencies/17708)
3. Content: which Saudi content rules and record-keeping duties apply to live video, archived YouTube embeds, profiles and chat? Who must take down content on each system, and what can this app promise when it cannot delete YouTube-hosted media? [Saudi audiovisual-media law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/ed5fdbc0-c183-4a8a-a8b7-a9ed004b5900/1)
4. Data location and transfer: what is the actual Supabase hosting region, backup location and subprocessor list; which Google/YouTube and map/image requests transfer personal data outside Saudi Arabia; what mechanism, notice and assessment are required? No Saudi-only residency claim is supported by the repository. [SDAIA transfer regulation](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/wcm/connect/e5bbede0-1119-4f70-b4ef-f043ce58d780/Regulation%2Bon%2BPersonal%2BData%2BTransfer%2BOutside%2Bthe%2BKingdom..pdf?CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE-e5bbede0-1119-4f70-b4ef-f043ce58d780-p6OMj1M&CONVERT_TO=url&MOD=AJPERES)
5. Privacy and terms: approve the English and Arabic controller identity, contact, purposes/legal bases, consent and withdrawal wording, public venues/coordinates, processors, data-subject request method, retention, breach, and deletion exceptions. Is the current privacy checkbox enough to establish acceptance of a separate UGC conduct policy before chat? [SDAIA privacy-policy guidance](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/ElaborationandDevelopingPrivacyPolicyGuideline/) [Google Play UGC policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937?hl=en-GB)
6. Minors: which age groups may use guest viewing, Google sign-in, chat or broadcasting? Is age assurance or guardian consent required? The app currently has no demonstrated age gate. The owner must select a truthful Play target audience; including children triggers extra Play requirements. [Google Play target-audience guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en)
7. Rights and retention: what data must be included in access/export, which report/moderator identities can be withheld, can a user withdraw consent without deleting the account, and what legal records survive deletion? Does deleting an org owner's account and its organization meet stakeholder obligations? [SDAIA rights summary](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPLCP) [SDAIA implementing regulation](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL2)
8. Store submission: after code and real-device evidence are complete, review final Data safety answers, account-deletion page, UGC controls, permission/FGS declaration, Play age/content rating and both listings. Google asks for accurate Data safety and a functional web deletion link when accounts can be created. [Play Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en) [Play account-deletion guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en)

Owner choices required before publication: target audience, supported streaming modes and access model, legal entity/contact, hosting region/transfer route, retention periods, safety staffing/contact, policy text, and the final listing name. Counsel should answer the legal questions; engineering should then test the resulting behavior.
