# Saudi compliance questions and evidence checklist

Status: 2026-09-24 preparation for counsel and owner. Every legal interpretation, applicability conclusion, retention period and proposed policy choice below is **for counsel review**. No certification or P8B acceptance is claimed.

SDAIA describes rights to be informed, access data, receive a readable copy, request correction/destruction and withdraw consent, subject to the law and regulations. [SDAIA rights summary](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPLCP) The implementing regulation addresses request handling, withdrawal, breach notice and records of processing. [SDAIA implementing regulation](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL2)
