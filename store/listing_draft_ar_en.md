## English

Name: Hadayah Live

Short description: Discover educational channels, lectures and venues in Arabic or English.

Full description:

Explore educational broadcaster profiles and organization pages in Hadayah Live. Find lectures by topic, browse linked YouTube videos, and view listed venues on a map.

Use the app in Arabic or English. Save lectures and follow channels with a signed-in account. When a live room is available, its discussion controls depend on the room and your account permissions.

Broadcasters and organizations can apply for review inside the app. Approval and available publishing controls depend on account status.
